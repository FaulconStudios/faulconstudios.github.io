Featured Tool: Procedural World Builder & Hydrology Weather

C# Source Included URP Ready Zero Art Dependencies Edit Mode Preview

A complete seeded 3D terrain, biome, river, settlement, and dynamic water-cycle weather framework. Runs out-of-the-box with runtime-synthesized biome textures, faceted 3D nature, flowing rivers, arched timber bridges, baked NavMesh, and 5-layer weather clouds—while letting you drop in your own prefabs and materials through ScriptableObject profiles.

7 Smooth-Blended Biomes

Meadowland, Dense Forest, Dirt Roads, Rocky Highlands, Dry Steppe, Sandy Beaches, and Snowy Peaks with optional custom material overrides.

Flowing Rivers & Bridges

Continuous curve-aligned river channels, calm lakes, Rigidbody buoyancy, and automatic river-aware road routing with arched timber bridges.

5-Layer Dynamic Cloud Cycle

Clouds grow from sunlit evaporation, coalesce into rainstorms, cast sun-projected directional light shadows, and dissipate once drained.

Physical Puddle Hydrology

Slope runoff traces downhill into concave basins, forms terrain-conforming puddles under rain clouds, and dries back into vapor in direct sun.

Built-In NavMesh & Audio Hooks

Automatic NavMesh baking with configurable water crossing costs, plus optional rain, wind, and distance-delayed thunder audio events.

Edit Mode Inspector & Demo

Generate, randomize, and lock world seeds directly in the Scene View, plus a ready-to-play demo with Free-Fly and Ground Walker modes.
